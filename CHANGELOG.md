Outreach refinement now receives the full voice guide with calibration samples explicitly marked as not reusable, after live testing showed the 3B model copying example sentences verbatim and dropping the recipient's own details.

The Shopify import now derives a price-free summary, a real category, materials, trades and business relevance from the export, retires products missing from a newer export, and never overwrites wording edited by hand. `product_enrichment.json` imports on top of it: 104 of its 110 handles matched the current catalogue, plus 10 trade knowledge notes and 4 past jobs. Products are ranked per request and per lead trade, five in full and seven in brief, with notes and past jobs alongside. Two rules are enforced in code rather than asked of the model: no pricing reaches a model or an email, and an unconfirmed job never carries its client's name. Measured live: "which products suit a physio clinic?" went from a name, a taxonomy path and a price to five real product names with a typical order each.
- Allowed a larger local model (`qwen3:8b`, `llama3.1:8b`, `gemma3:12b`) to be chosen in Settings, defaulting to `llama3.2:3b` so nothing changes until a model is pulled. The small model is always used while LightBurn or xTool is running. Added a Knowledge tab for editing trade notes and confirming past jobs, an "Enrich pending products" run for products the pack does not cover, and a draft edit rate on Overview that measures how much outreach gets rewritten before approval.
- Added optional, disabled-by-default Anthropic draft polish for one reviewed email at a time, with a DPAPI-backed key, model allowlist, narrow lead/draft context, price stripping, structured response handling, audit metadata, and no mutation of the persisted draft. The metric gate and real-key manual check remain operator follow-up; see [the implementation handover](docs/implementation/CLOUD_DRAFT_POLISH_HANDOVER.md).
