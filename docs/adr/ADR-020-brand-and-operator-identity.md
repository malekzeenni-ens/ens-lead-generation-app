# ADR-020: Brand and operator identity for the local assistants

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Every local AI surface answered like a stock `llama3.2:3b` assistant. The prompts described the application in detail but never described the business it exists to sell for, the person operating it, or how Etch 'N' Shine is allowed to sound. The result was generic, often US-spelled output that had to be rewritten by hand — most visibly in outreach refinement, where the model produced budget-gift-site copy, and in general chat, where it could not answer who the operator is or what the business sells.
- **Decision:** Add `app/domains/brand/profile.py` as the single source of truth for business facts, operator profile and voice rules, and inject it into every prompt builder. The content mirrors the `etch-n-shine` brand-voice reference so the app and the wider toolchain cannot drift apart. `BRAND_PROFILE_VERSION` is recorded on assistant audit events alongside the prompt version.
- **Tiering:** The identity is served at three tiers because the standard context window is 8,192 tokens and halves to 4,096 in the protected profile, where the workspace snapshot already consumes most of it. `BRIEF` (~300 tokens) steers word choice only and is used for structured JSON tasks, the campaign planner, lead briefings, autofill, the stalled-lead digest, and general chat while resources are protected. `CORE` (~1,100 tokens) adds the full business and operator picture for open conversation. `WRITING` (~1,500 tokens) adds tone principles for copy a prospect will read, and is used only by outreach refinement. The lead filter translator gets no identity at all: mapping a phrase onto dropdown values needs no voice, and the window is better spent elsewhere.
- **Calibration samples, not reusable phrases:** A 3B model reproduces worked examples verbatim. Live testing showed it lifting whole example sentences into outreach emails and dropping the recipient's own details to do it. The writing tier therefore labels its examples as register samples and forbids copying any fragment of them, and the checklist asks whether each sentence was built from supplied facts.
- **No prices, no counts:** The profile carries no price, price range or discount, and no count of products or anything else. Pricing is the operator's to give once he knows the job, and a count written into a prompt goes stale silently while the live snapshot stays authoritative. Outreach refinement states explicitly that stripping money overrides its own instruction to preserve concrete facts — without that precedence the model kept the figures from the draft it was handed. `tests/test_brand_identity.py` fails if a price or the missing rule reappears.
- **Second person:** The operator profile names Malek so the model can answer "who am I", then instructs it to address him as "you". Naming him without that instruction made the copilot reply in the third person and advise the reader to "ask Malek about pricing".
- **Prompt-injection boundary:** Identity is instruction-grade content and lives in the system prompt, above the workspace snapshot. It is deliberately not added to the snapshot JSON, which remains untrusted data the model is told never to follow.
- **Limitations:** Identity steers tone and factual grounding; it does not make a 3B model reliable. It still occasionally invents a number that is not in the snapshot, and it does not always attach the requested one-line reason to each recommended action. All AI output remains advisory and reviewable. Changing the profile changes every assistant at once, so `BRAND_PROFILE_VERSION` must be bumped to keep audit records meaningful.
- **Alternatives considered:** Duplicating voice rules into each prompt, which guarantees drift; a fine-tuned local model, which is far more work than a prompt and much harder to correct; storing the profile in workspace settings, which adds a migration and a settings surface before we know which parts actually need tuning; injecting the full voice guide everywhere, which does not fit the protected window.
- **Rollback:** Call `identity_block` nowhere, or pass `IdentityTier.BRIEF` throughout. The prompt builders keep working and no stored data depends on the profile.
